Privacy

We keepthe minimum.

SolFlag has no accounts, no cookies and no ads. What you save on the site stays in your browser. Here is everything that happens with your data, with no small print.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Versión en español

Who is responsible

SolFlag is a project by El Gato Trade. For anything about your data, write to solflag.team@gmail.com.

The website (solflag.com)

WhatWhere and why
Your lists, Confluence groups, risk history, language and settingsOnly in your browser (local storage), so you find them when you come back. They never reach us. They go when you clear the site's data in your browser.
Your RPC URL (for example from Helius), if you add oneOnly in your browser. It is used to talk to that service from your browser; it never goes through us.
AnalyticsWe count visits and how often each feature is used (scan, whales, map, going back, share, open the bot) with GoatCounter, without cookies. It records the page (if you arrive through a token or map link, it includes that token's address; a wallet's address, never), where you came from, your browser, screen size and approximate country. We do not keep your IP or the wallets you analyse.
Analysed tokensWhen you analyse a token, the site tells our API (api.solflag.com, on Cloudflare) which token it was. We keep only the token address, how many times it has been analysed and when, to study later who is behind it. We do not keep who analysed it or your IP, and wallets are never reported. Cloudflare limits how many reports come from one connection without us storing the IP.
HostingThe site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log your IP for technical and security reasons.
FontsServed from solflag.com: your browser does not request them from Google.

To analyse a token or wallet, your browser asks public services directly: market data (DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, CoinGecko), RugCheck, Solana nodes (PublicNode, Solana Vibe Station or your own) and jsDelivr and cdnjs (the code of the engine and the map). They see your IP and the address you look up, and have their own privacy policies.

The Telegram bot (@SolFlagBot)

To send you alerts, the bot keeps for each chat: its ID, the language, what you watch (tokens and wallets) and your price alerts. It does not keep your name or phone number. It runs on a Bot-Hosting server.

To delete all of it, send /delete to the bot (it asks you to confirm). Telegram handles the data of its own app separately.

Public on-chain data

Solana addresses and transactions are public. SolFlag publishes data about addresses (the launch registry, known faces and the wallet base): facts visible on chain, such as how many launches a wallet paid for and how they ended, never personal labels or accusations. The registry can be downloaded on Data and accuracy.

Some company wallets carry a name, such as exchange wallets (Binance, Coinbase…), when their address is public and verified. We do not put names on people.

If an address is yours and you think something about it is wrong, write to solflag.team@gmail.com: we will review it and correct any error.

Why, and for how long

  • Analytics and on-chain data: legitimate interest in improving the site and helping people spot risks.
  • Bot: to provide the service you ask for. What it keeps stays while you use it or until you delete it.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone, and there are no ads.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or portability of your data by writing to solflag.team@gmail.com. If you think we are not handling it properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

If this policy changes, you will see it here with the new date.
