Follow the usage, not the noise.

"Usage" is each group's own measure: fees charged over 30 days; for stablecoins, how much circulates on Solana; for real-world assets, the tokenized value (on Solana, or the total across all chains for Ondo, Securitize and Remora, as each row says; xStocks is computed on Solana: minted supply minus what the issuer's accounts hold). For memes, AI and games without fees, their token's market cap. "vs the market": how much it changed over 30 days compared with its whole market on Solana (fees: ; stablecoins: ); for the rest it will come from our own daily history once it is a month old. "Holders" and "traders": how many wallets hold the token and how many traded in 24 h; "organic" are buyers Jupiter doesn't flag as bots. "Value × revenue": how many times its token is worth what the protocol earns in a year.

Where the data comes fromDefiLlama: fees, revenue, deposits and stablecoins on Solana. CoinGecko: prices and market cap. Jupiter: holders and traders. The chain (Helius): the value of xStocks on Solana. Updated daily.
The top 10 of each groupNot the 50 biggest earners on all of Solana: that way a small group (oracles, games, bridges) isn't left out for being small.
When a project launches a tokenIf a project that showed up without a token appears with one, it moves to "Just launched a token". Copies with the same name usually follow: check the address on its official website and run it through the scanner.
