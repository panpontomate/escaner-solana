What a rug pull on Solana is and how to spot it

A rug pull is when whoever is behind a token takes the money and leaves buyers holding something worthless. Almost all of them leave a sign on-chain before it happens. These are the most common kinds and how to see them.

1

Pulling the liquidity

The creator puts money into the pool and, once people have bought in, takes it out. Without liquidity nobody can sell.

The sign: the liquidity isn't locked or burned in a market only days old. On SolFlag it shows amber below 60% locked and red below 20%, in markets younger than 90 days.

2

Minting more and selling it

If the mint authority is still active, the creator can print millions of new tokens out of nothing and sell them into the pool.

The sign: «Mint Authority» active and owned by a person. One of the most serious: nothing else is needed to take your money.

3

Dumping what they kept

The creator or their group keep a big part of the supply (sometimes split across several wallets to hide it) and sell all at once when the price goes up.

The sign: high concentration among the largest people wallets, or several big ones funded from the same wallet. Afterwards, SolFlag's chart marks when the creator and the launch buyers sold.

4

Freezing you so you can't sell

With the freeze authority active they can lock your balance: you can buy but not sell. The same happens with transfer fees raised up to 100%.

The sign: «Freeze Authority» active on a memecoin, or Token-2022 extensions like transfer fees or transfer hooks.

5

Launch, abandon, launch again

The most common one: farms that launch dozens of tokens a day, each with a new creator, collect what comes in and move on to the next. On 15 Nov we saw one wallet with 48 launches in a day and another with 388 in total, 367 of which died within 24 hours.

The sign: whoever paid for its creation launched 2 or more tokens in 24 hours, or most of their previous tokens went nowhere. Since they change creators, check who paid and who gave the creator their first SOL.

6

Faking a market that isn't there

Inflated liquidity (a pool showing millions with a few real dollars behind it) or volume made by the same wallets buying and selling to fake interest.

The sign: the real side of the pool is less than 5% of what is shown, or 80% or more of the volume is round trips.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get money back from a rug pull?

Almost never. Solana transactions can't be undone and whoever does it usually moves the money fast. That is why it pays to check the signs before buying.

Can a token with locked liquidity still be a rug pull?

Yes: even if the liquidity can't be pulled, the creator can dump what they kept, mint more if the mint authority is active, or freeze balances.

Is every token that dies a rug pull?

No. Most new tokens die because nobody buys them, with no trap involved. A rug pull is when an insider takes the money; the signs in this guide are what make it possible.

Is this financial advice?

No. SolFlag is a guidance tool: it explains what can be seen on-chain and doesn't recommend buying or selling.
