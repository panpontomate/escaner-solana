Every flag,explained.

From signals to the five tiers

The four traffic lights still turn green, amber or red depending on the worst signal they find inside. The token's tier comes from looking at the warnings one by one and at what kind they are, not from adding up points: that way a single trap is not diluted by many good things. Since Nov 14 there are five tiers, based on what the token is.

Controlwho can mint more, freeze or charge you to move it
Concentrationhow much the largest wallets of people hold
Marketwhether there is real liquidity and you can get out
Dependencywhether it depends on an issuer, a bridge or a program

↓ EACH WARNING IS LOOKED AT ONE BY ONE ↓

Diamonda big project on our hand-reviewed list (Jupiter, Jito, Pyth…) or a trusted issuer (Circle, Paxos…). Being on the list is enough; we review by hand who gets in.
Greenno warning at all and 7 days or more on the market. A memecoin can be green; never diamond.
Yellowone mild warning or something unchecked. Also every token under 7 days old: it is the best a newborn can get.
Orangedanger without a trap: one serious warning that is not a trap (very low liquidity, a few wallets holding almost everything) or two or more mild ones.
Reda trap (the creator can mint more or pull the liquidity, launches in series, fake liquidity or volume, a copy of another token) or two serious warnings.

Why a newborn never goes above yellow. After hours or a few days a token has proven nothing yet: in the registry, 99% of the ones we see die in their first week. It can be orange or red from the first minute; green, only from day 7.

Red and orange almost always fail at launch (99.4% and 99.6% dead within a day in the registry). The difference is not how long they last but the kind of danger: in red, someone can take your money or you are being deceived.

Traffic light 1

Who controls the token

The first thing is to know whether someone can change the rules after you buy: mint more tokens, freeze your balance or charge you to move it. We read it straight from the chain.

SignalTurns on when…Why
More can be mintedthe mint key is active and belongs to a personif it belongs to a program (a bridge, a protocol), it goes to Dependencywhoever holds it can dilute your share to zero in a second
Can be frozenthe freeze key is active and belongs to a personit can stop you from selling; digital dollars carry it by law, which is why it is amber
Transfer feethe token charges on every transferthe fee can go up; with a known issuer and at 0% it does not count (PYUSD, USDG)
Transfer hook · Born frozen · Non-transferableToken-2022 extensions that are really seta hook with no program and no owner does nothing and is not flaggedthey change what you can do with your balance
Serial launcheswhoever paid for its creation launched 2 or more tokens in 24 h (red with 5 or more) in 100 Pump.fun tokens, one wallet launched 7 copies of «GMS» in 10 minutes
Creator historyof their 3 or more previous tokens, 80% never took off (red: 10 or more and 95%) 86% of new tokens never reach $10,000, so one in ten taking off is already better than chance: that is why red asks for so much
Copy of a famous token or a branduses the symbol of USDC, SOL… with another address, or the exact name of a real companyit is built to look like something it is not: the level goes to high even if the rest looks fine
Same name as an older tokenthere is an older token with the same name and symbol and a bigger market (red if it has 10 times more liquidity) someone searching «X» can buy the copy; a relaunch with a bigger market than the original is not flagged
Issuer keys changedan official token of a verified issuer has a key that was not on its listit can be a normal rotation or someone who took it: until it is checked at the official source, never green

Traffic light 2

Holder concentration

How much of the supply the largest wallets hold. Before counting, every large account is traced to its owner: a pool, a program or an exchange is not a person about to sell.

SignalTurns on when…Why
Highly concentratedone wallet holds more than 30% or the top 10 more than 70%one person selling sinks the price
Concentratedone wallet more than 15% or the top 10 more than 50%the same, with less margin
Named exchangesshown, but not countedit is their users' money, not one person's
Named market makersshown and countedWintermute and DWF Labs, with addresses published by them or by Solscan—they can sell a lot at once

For digital dollars, stock tokens and bridged tokens, red drops to amber. With a verified issuer, the large wallets are its treasury, custodians and exchanges, and they do not count: PYUSD had 56% in a single one.

Traffic light 3

Market and exit

Having a price is not enough: you need to be able to sell. Here we look at how much real money is behind it, whether someone can pull it and whether the trading is real.

SignalTurns on when…Why
Low liquidityunder $10,000 (red) or $50,000 (amber) selling a normal amount moves the price a lot
Inflated liquiditythe real side of the pool (SOL, USDC or USDT) is under 5% of what is reporteda «Shopify» reported $12.9M with $52 really behind it; at 100 times or more, the level goes to high
Unlocked liquidityunder 20% locked or burned (amber under 60%), only for markets under 90 days the creator can take the pool's money; in veteran tokens many provide it and it is not a signal (JUP scored worse than a new memecoin)
Inflated volume80% or more of the volume comes from wallets that buy and sell the same (red if they are also 25 or fewer and the top 3 move 75%) 14 trending tokens came out between 0 and 47%; a 6-minute-old pool, 96%. Volume/liquidity does not work: healthy memecoins trade 20 to 360 times their liquidity
Low volumeunder $1,000 in 24 hnobody is buying or selling it
New marketunder 7 dayswith no history there is nothing to check
Your position is heavywhat the analysed wallet holds is 10% or more of the liquidityyou could not sell it all without sinking the price

What is not flagged. On the Pump.fun curve and in concentrated-liquidity pools there is no liquidity to lock: RugCheck reports 0% and flagged 49 of 75 curve tokens in red. This rule does not apply there.

If it is still on the Pump.fun curve. Under the level a box shows what really happens there (registry Nov 1–12, 8,767 tokens): fewer than 2 in 100 ever graduate and, of those that do, 9 in 10 die within a day. If it is rising very fast (worth $15,000 or more, gaining $5,000 or more per minute of life, and no serial launcher behind it), it says so: more than half of those that moved like this graduated, checked on days not used to build the rule. It is context: it does not change the level, and graduating is not doing well.

Traffic light 4

Dependency on an issuer

Some tokens are worth what someone's promise is worth: Circle's dollar, the share xStocks holds in custody or the bridge that keeps the original. That is not bad, but it is worth knowing.

SignalTurns on when…Why
Trusted issuerall its keys belong to an issuer on our short list (Circle, Tether…)its freeze and mint keys are how the product works
Known issuerits keys belong to a verified issuer that is not on the short list (Ondo, xStocks…)it depends on the issuer keeping its word; addresses come from its official website, never from aggregators
Controlled by a programthe mint or freeze key belongs to a program (bridge, protocol)if the program fails or is attacked, so is the token
Permanent delegatesomeone can move or burn tokens from any walletnormal for tokenized stocks; not for a memecoin

Who is behind it

Apart from the traffic lights, SolFlag records who paid for the creation of each token, who created it and who gave the creator their first SOL. That shows what a single token cannot.

  • A group that has been in many launches. Wallets that send SOL to each other and have been behind, or among the first buyers of, 5 or more launches. With 2 matches it can be chance, so it is not shown.
  • Buys at launch. Wallets that buy in the first blocks of 3 or more launches. It is not a token signal: there is one in 84% of launches, and with them 98.6% die within 24 h, without them 99.3%. What matters is whether they still hold tokens to sell, and that shows among the largest wallets.
  • Names that describe what it does, not accusations: «Pays for launches», «Hands out SOL», «Creates tokens in series», «Exchange-like activity». Always with the reason in numbers.

What it cannot know

  • Intent. SolFlag sees facts on the chain, not what someone plans to do. A green token can fall and a red one can rise.
  • What could not be checked. If a source does not answer, the light shows «not checked» and the token does not go above yellow. If nothing can be read, it shows «No data», never «Probable spam».
  • What happens outside Solana. A bridged or exchange token can have its market elsewhere: with little liquidity here it shows amber, not red.

It is not a recommendation to buy or sell. How accurate it is, misses included, is in Data and accuracy. If you see a mistake, write to solflag.team@gmail.com.

Changes to the rules

When a rule gives a wrong result, it is changed and noted here with the reason.

  • 11/14Five tiers instead of three: diamond, green, yellow, orange and red. High and medium came out almost the same on new tokens (99.4% and 99.3% dead within a day); now red is a trap and orange is danger without a trap. A newborn token does not go above yellow until day 7.
  • 11/14Inflated volume. We look at who trades in the last ~300 trades, not at volume over liquidity, which gave false positives.
  • 11/14«No data» instead of «Probable spam» when the chain does not answer while reading a token with no market.
  • 11/14Buys at launch: a wallet label, not a token signal (almost the same share dies with or without them).
  • 11/12Verified issuers with their token list. A new key on an official token shows amber until it is checked.
  • 11/12A known issuer's token with no market shows «No market», not «Probable spam» (Ondo's OUSG and USDon).
  • 11/1Concentrated-liquidity pools left out of the unlocked liquidity rule ($STONK showed red with $2.4M in 27 pairs).
  • 11/1The real USDC and USDT no longer show as a copy of an older «USDC».
