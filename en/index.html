How to get a Helius URL (free, 2 minutes)
  1. Go to helius.dev and create a free account. If you care about privacy, use a secondary email.
  2. In the dashboard, find your MainnetURL. It starts with https://mainnet.helius-rpc.com/?api-key= and ends with your key.
  3. Copy all of it and paste it in the field above. Nothing else to click: it is saved when you analyse.

With it, searches are several times faster and you can estimate whether a wallet wins or loses. Do not share it: it contains your key.

The 5 flags

The signals that matter.

Each flag shows green, amber or red, always with its reason in one sentence.

01 / Creator

Whether they can mint more tokens or freeze yours.

02 / Concentration

A few wallets holding a lot, even when they hide in groups.

03 / Liquidity

Real, inflated or able to be pulled at once.

04 / Dependency

Bridges and programs you have to trust.

05 / Launch

Bundles at launch and serial launchers.
